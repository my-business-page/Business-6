The photographer

From film
to feeling.

Hafiz Jumain began his photography journey with a film camera, later moving through Nikon digital SLRs and into a practice shaped by weddings, events, landscapes and fine art.

The work has always been about more than technical perfection: slowing down, watching, and recognising the instant when an ordinary scene becomes something worth keeping.

The philosophy

Look longer.
Feel more.
